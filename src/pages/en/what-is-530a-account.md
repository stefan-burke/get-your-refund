  - type: faqs
    items:
      - question: Why do 530A accounts exist?
        answer: |
          The 530A program was created to help children have some savings when they become adults. The money may help pay for things like college, buying a home, or certain emergencies later in life.
      - question: Why is it called a “Trump Account”?
        answer: |
          Congress named these accounts “Trump Accounts” when they were created in July 2025. The name was meant to connect the policy to the Trump administration. Despite the name, the President is not personally involved with these accounts. The accounts are not run by his companies, and the money does not come from his personal funds. A similar example is how people refer to health insurance policies created in 2010 as “Obamacare.”
      - question: When will 530A accounts open?
        answer: |
          530A accounts are available now.

          [See our page on how to sign up](/en/how-to-signup/)
      - question: What if I have more than one child?
        answer: |
          Each 530A account is for an individual child. You must open a separate account for each of your children. You can’t create a pooled 530A for your family.
      - question: I’ve heard 530A referred to as a “retirement account.” Why?
        answer: |
          Once your child turns 18, their 530A becomes an [Individual Retirement Account (IRA)](https://www.fidelity.com/learning-center/smart-money/what-is-an-ira), a common type of retirement savings account. Like all IRAs, they can access the money before they retire, but it must be used for certain things to avoid paying a penalty.

          [Read more about these limits here](/en/how-530a-account-works/).
